The VocalFluxStudio Process

One brief.
One managed workflow.

From your first project brief to final delivery, our production team coordinates the people, resources, schedules and quality checks required to move your project forward.

How It Works

A clear production journey with defined stages and controlled communication.

01

Submit Your Project

Tell us about the project, services required, source and target languages, duration, versions, expected delivery and budget indication.

Project Brief
02

NDA & Secure File Submission

Where required, project confidentiality is established before sensitive materials are shared. Files are handled through controlled project access.

NDA / Controlled Access
03

Production Assessment

Our team reviews the scope, identifies the production requirements and determines the resources, workflow and timeline needed.

Planning
04

Commercial Discussion & Quote

The project scope is discussed and the commercial proposal is prepared. Your stated budget is an input for discussion—not an automatic final quote.

Commercials
05

Approval & Production Setup

Once the scope and commercial terms are approved, the production team schedules the work and coordinates the required artists, studios and specialists.

Production Planning
06

Production

Work moves through the agreed production pipeline—dubbing, editing, DI, VFX, animation, sound or complete post-production as required.

In Production
07

Quality Control

Outputs are reviewed against the project's technical and creative requirements before they move to client review.

QC
08

Client Review

Review versions are shared through the project workflow. Feedback can be organized against the relevant version and, where applicable, timecode.

Review / Revisions
09

Final Delivery

After final approval, the approved deliverables are prepared according to the agreed delivery requirements and released through controlled delivery.

Final Delivery

Choose How You Work With Us

VocalFluxStudio can adapt the production model to your project.

Managed

Give us the brief and let our production team plan and coordinate the required resources from start to finish.

Client-Selected

Where appropriate, you can specify preferred professionals, studios or production resources while we manage the project workflow.

Hybrid

Combine your preferred resources with VocalFluxStudio's production coordination, QC and delivery management.

Important: VocalFluxStudio is designed as a managed production partner, not an open freelancer marketplace. Client communication and project coordination remain within the production workflow.
